# Focus room and neighbors

On the floor plan (<LIV code="floor:map" />), isolate a room and its neighbors to focus on one area.

<CloudinaryAsset publicId="help/changelog/v3.5.21/focus-room-neighbors-en" kind="video" asGif width={640} gifFps={8} alt="Focus on a room and its neighbors on the floor plan" />

## Prerequisites

- Floor plan open on the relevant level.

## Steps

1. **Open the floor plan**  
   Scenario starting point: <LIV code="floor:map" /> view.

2. **Click on the Fit plan to view button**  
   Adjusts zoom to show the whole plan.

3. **Click on the Show only neighboring rooms button**  
   *Hides the rest of the plan to focus on a room and its neighbors.*

## Gestures without narration

- Click a **room** to choose it as the focus centre.
- Use depths **1**, **2** or **3** to widen or narrow the neighbourhood shown.
