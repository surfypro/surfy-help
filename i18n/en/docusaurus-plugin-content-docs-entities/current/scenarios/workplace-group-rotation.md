# Group rotation on the floor plan

On a floor plan (<LIV code="floor:map" />), rotate **several workstations and/or objects at once** after selecting them together.

<CloudinaryAsset publicId="help/changelog/v3.5.23/mixed-group-rotation-en" kind="video" asGif width={640} gifFps={8} alt="Group rotation of workstations and objects in plan edit mode" />

## Prerequisites

- Edit rights on the floor plan.
- Floor plan open on the relevant level.

## Steps

1. **Open the floor plan**  
   Scenario starting point: <LIV code="floor:map" /> view.

2. **Click on the Enable plan edit mode button**  
   Switches the plan to edit mode so you can move and rotate workstations and objects (shortcut **e**).

3. **Select a workstation or an object** (first shape)  
   That shape becomes the active selection.

4. **Hold the Shift key**  
   Lets you add more workstations and/or objects to the selection.

5. **Select another shape** (workstation or object)  
   At least two shapes are selected; the group selection frame appears.

## Gestures without narration

- On the **top edge** of the frame: move, duplicate (if enabled), or **rotate** via the rotation handle.
- Drag the rotation handle: **all** selected shapes (workstations and objects) rotate **around the selection centre**, keeping their relative layout.
- Release **Shift** when you no longer need the multi-selection.

## Single shape rotation

When **one** workstation or **one** object is selected, the rotation handle stays on that shape.

## Tip

- Hold **Shift** while rotating for a **free angle**.
- Without Shift, rotation snaps to **45°** steps.
- To **snap sides** between workstations or objects, turn on [Segment magnetism on the floor plan](./workplace-segment-magnetism).

## Limits

- The building **3D** view is not included.
